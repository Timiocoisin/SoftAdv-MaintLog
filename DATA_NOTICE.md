# Data notice

## Included in this repository

- Protocol / operating-point tables under `release_tables/` (CSV/JSON summaries used to support manuscript boards).
- Research source code under `training/` and `experiments/`.

These materials are provided for research verification and editorial assessment. They do **not** by themselves constitute a complete from-scratch reconstruction of every training run.

## Not included

- Raw water-wall laboratory image frames
- Feature-cache binaries
- Model checkpoints / weights
- Pass-level prediction dumps from historical exploratory runs
- Manuscript LaTeX/PDF and submission correspondence

## Access route

Until a public frame/checkpoint release upon manuscript acceptance, laboratory frames and checkpoints remain available from the corresponding author on reasonable request for reproducibility checks.
